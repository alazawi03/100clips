100clips - Notifications
========================
Updated: 9 October 2026

Status key:
  [BUILT]    working in the app today (demo data)
  [PLANNED]  agreed, not built yet (phone push needs the backend)

Messages (chats) are NOT notifications: they have their own tab / page with an unread count,
so they are never shown twice.


------------------------------------------------------------
1. CREATOR APP - the bell on Home  [BUILT]
------------------------------------------------------------
Where: bell icon at the top of Home, with the number of new ones.
Layout: grouped under Today / Yesterday / This week / Earlier, newest first.
Each row: an icon for the kind, the brand's logo, what happened, which ad, the time.
New ones have a pink dot. Tapping opens that ad.

  #  When                         Arabic                                   English                              Details shown
  1  Brand sends an offer         عرض جديد من {brand}                      New offer from {brand}               ad · format · price
  2  Brand ships the product      {brand} أرسلت لك المنتج                  {brand} sent you the product         ad · how it's delivered
  3  Brand asks for a revision    {brand} طلبت تعديل على مقطعك             {brand} asked for a revision         the brand's note
  4  Brand approves the clip      {brand} اعتمدت مقطعك                     {brand} approved your clip           money received · stars
  5  Brand cancels the offer      {brand} ألغت العرض                       {brand} cancelled the offer          ad

Also on approval [BUILT]: the full-screen celebration opens by itself, once per approval,
when the app opens or comes back to the front, and only while Home is on screen.
